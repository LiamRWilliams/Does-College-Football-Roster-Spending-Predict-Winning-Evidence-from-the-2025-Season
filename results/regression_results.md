# Regression Results

All reported standard errors are heteroskedasticity-robust.

| Specification | Outcome | Payroll coefficient | 95% CI | p-value | R² |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Baseline, 68 teams | Wins | 0.206 | [0.110, 0.301] | <0.001 | 0.164 |
| Conference-controlled, 67 Power 4 teams | Wins | 0.315 | [0.163, 0.467] | <0.001 | 0.217 |
| Baseline, 68 teams | Point differential | 11.015 | [6.478, 15.551] | <0.001 | 0.202 |
| Conference-controlled, 67 Power 4 teams | Point differential | 15.358 | [8.042, 22.674] | <0.001 | 0.238 |

## Interpretation

In the baseline model, an additional **$1 million** in estimated roster payroll is associated with about **0.21 additional regular-season wins**.

In the preferred conference-controlled robustness specification, restricted to ACC, Big 12, Big Ten, and SEC teams, an additional **$1 million** in estimated roster payroll is associated with about **0.32 additional wins** and **15.4 additional points of season point differential**.

These estimates describe **associations, not causal effects**.
